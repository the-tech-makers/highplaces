Grand Traverse of the Indian Himalaya

A high-altitude crossing of Ladakh and the remote Changthang Plateau — monasteries, glacial passes above 5,000m and the nomadic pastures around Tso Kar and Tso Moriri, led by a guide with over 40 years in the Himalaya.

Overview

Ladakh, often called the Land of High Passes, sits high in the Indian Himalaya — a Union Territory known for dramatic scenery, windswept valleys and monasteries built into cliffsides, and one of the last places where Tibetan Buddhism is still widely practised.

The Grand Traverse of the Indian Himalaya is a high-altitude trekking adventure through the remote Changthang Plateau. After time in Leh to acclimatise, the route combines gradual altitude gain, cultural stops at iconic monasteries, and a rewarding multi-day trek across high mountain passes, glacial lakes and traditional nomadic pasturelands.

This trip suits experienced trekkers comfortable camping at altitude for over a week, who want Ladakh's culture and its wildest, least-visited corners in a single itinerary.

This trek is led by Valerie Parkinson, who has been leading treks and expeditions in the Himalaya for over 40 years. In 2008 she became the first British woman to summit Manaslu (8,163m) without supplementary oxygen, and spends most summers — and many winters — guiding in Ladakh.

Why you'll love this trip

  • Genuine remote wilderness — days on the Changthang Plateau with almost no other trekkers.
  • Culture and altitude balanced with careful acclimatisation days built in.
  • A small group, led by one of the most experienced Ladakh guides in the UK.

Trip Highlights

  • Spectacular flight over the Himalaya from Delhi to Leh, with panoramic views of glaciers and peaks.
  • Visit iconic monasteries including Thiksey and Hemis.
  • Cross multiple high-altitude passes, including Khardung La.
  • Marvel at snow-capped Himalayan landscapes across the Changthang Plateau.
  • Spot rare wildlife — black-necked cranes, Brahminy ducks, kiang, marmots, wolves and Tibetan antelope.
  • Explore the nomadic village of Korzok and the azure waters of Tso Moriri Lake.

Trip Notes

Who is this trip for?

Trekkers aged 18 and over who are comfortable camping for around a week at altitudes above 4,500m, with multiple pass crossings over 5,000m. Good general fitness and prior trekking experience are recommended given the remoteness of the Changthang route.

Altitude

The trek reaches a maximum altitude of around 5,400m at the Kyamaru La and Yalung Naula Pass. Several days are built in around Leh (3,500m) specifically for acclimatisation before the high-altitude trekking days begin.

Your trek leader

Led by Valerie Parkinson, who has guided in the Himalaya for over 40 years and spends most summers in Ladakh — see the Overview section above for her full background.
