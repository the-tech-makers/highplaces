Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about selecting the right Himalayan route, difficulty ratings, durations, and private itineraries.

What should I do for my first Himalayan trek?

Consider a classic teahouse route such as the Poon Hill trek or the Langtang Valley trek. These are easy-to-moderate difficulty and typically run 5 to 8 days, making them well suited to first-time trekkers.

Which Himalayan trek is best for a high-altitude challenge?

Consider a high-pass route such as Everest Base Camp or the Annapurna Circuit. These are challenging treks typically lasting 12 to 18 days, best suited to experienced trekkers with good fitness.

Which trek combines culture and trekking in the Himalayas?

Consider a heritage trail through monasteries and villages, such as Upper Mustang or Tsum Valley. These moderate-difficulty treks typically run 7 to 12 days.

Which trip is best for wildlife and gentle walking in Nepal?

Consider a gentle nature walk through a national park such as Chitwan or the Sagarmatha buffer zone. These are easy trips typically lasting 3 to 6 days.

Can I join a small-group Himalayan trek?

Yes, fixed-departure group treks are available with 4 to 10 like-minded travellers. Difficulty and duration vary by route.

Can I get a completely private, tailor-made Himalayan itinerary?

Yes, a fully tailor-made itinerary can be built around your preferred route, pace and dates. Duration and difficulty are flexible.

How long do Himalayan adventures typically take?

Duration depends on the route and style. The options in this guide range from short 3–6 day wildlife and walking trips to challenging high-altitude adventures of around 12–18 days. Tailor-made itineraries can be flexible.

How do I choose the right difficulty level?

Start with your trekking experience, fitness and the type of experience you want. Easy options include first-trek routes and gentle wildlife walks; moderate routes add more trekking and cultural exploration; challenging routes are designed around high-altitude objectives.

Are Poon Hill and Langtang Valley suitable for beginners?

They are presented in this guide as classic easy-to-moderate teahouse routes and good choices for first-time Himalayan trekkers. Typical trips run for about 5–8 days.

Which routes are included among the high-altitude challenges?

The guide highlights Everest Base Camp and the Annapurna Circuit as examples of high-pass, challenging Himalayan routes, typically lasting 12–18 days.

Which routes are good for culture and heritage?

Upper Mustang and Tsum Valley are highlighted as heritage-focused options, combining trekking with monasteries, villages and cultural experiences. Typical duration is 7–12 days.

Where can I combine wildlife with gentle walking?

The guide points to Chitwan and the Sagarmatha buffer zone for gentle nature walks and wildlife-focused experiences, typically lasting 3–6 days.

How many people are in a small-group trek?

The small-group option described in this guide is a fixed-departure experience with approximately 4–10 like-minded travellers. Difficulty and duration vary by route.

Can the route, pace and dates be customized?

Yes. The private itinerary option is fully tailor-made, allowing the route, pace and dates to be built around the traveller. Duration and difficulty are flexible.
