Have a question?

Who are High Places? +

High Places is an independent UK-based adventure tour operator specialising in travel across India, Nepal, Bhutan and Sri Lanka.

Where is High Places based? +

High Places is based in Sheffield, United Kingdom.

What countries does High Places operate in? +

High Places currently offers adventures across India, Nepal, Bhutan and Sri Lanka.

Can High Places arrange tailor-made trips? +

Yes. Tailor-made journeys can be created around your interests, travel dates, preferred experiences and travel style.

Are High Places trips suitable for solo travellers? +

Our small-group adventures can provide an excellent way for solo travellers to explore with other like-minded people. Specific arrangements vary by trip.

How do I book a High Places adventure? +

Start by exploring our adventures or contact the High Places team to discuss the journey you have in mind.
